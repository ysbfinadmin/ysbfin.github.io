03 / Core Research Pillars

Four Core Research &
Educational Pillars

Our faculty conduct theoretical and empirical research across four primary domains: Corporate Finance, Asset Pricing, Financial Institutions & Derivatives, and Insurance & Risk Management.

Corporate Finance & Governance

기업재무 및 지배구조

Theoretical and empirical investigation of corporate financial decisions aimed at firm value maximization, including capital raising, capital structure, payout policy, IPOs, mergers and acquisitions (M&A), corporate governance, ESG, and innovation (R&D) investment.

Key Topics Capital Structure · M&A & Restructuring · Corporate Governance · ESG & Innovation · Internal Capital Markets

Asset Pricing & Investments

자산가격결정 및 투자론

In-depth research on pricing mechanisms across equities, fixed income, foreign exchange, and real estate, alongside capital market efficiency, market microstructure, macro- and international finance dynamics, investment strategies, and institutional performance evaluation.

Key Topics Asset Pricing Theory · Empirical Finance · Portfolio Strategy · Macro & International Finance · Market Liquidity

Financial Institutions & Derivatives

금융기관 및 파생상품

Rigorous exploration of derivatives pricing theory (futures, options, swaps) and risk management, management and regulation of financial institutions, credit risk modeling, and capital market microstructure.

Key Topics Derivatives Pricing · Banking & Regulation · Credit Risk · Risk Management · Market Microstructure

Insurance & Risk Management

보험 및 리스크 관리

Research on core challenges in the financial and insurance sectors grounded in economic, statistical, and legal principles, including insurance contract design, enterprise risk management, pension markets, longevity risk, financial regulation, and reinsurance.

Key Topics Risk Management · Actuarial Science & Pensions · Financial Regulation · Private Credit & Stability · Reinsurance
